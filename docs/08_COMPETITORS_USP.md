## Norvex's USP / moat
1. **Natural-language → personalized structured product.** Competitors ship fixed products; Norvex builds
   one to *your* goal and risk in plain English, at $100 minimum.
2. **Provable safety, not marketing.** The principal-protection limit is enforced by deterministic
   Monte-Carlo + 6 guardrails (and, once R5.3 ships, an **on-chain assertion**). The LLM cannot breach it.
3. **Data flywheel.** Every goal, debate transcript, and outcome is logged (`decisions`) — a proprietary
   dataset competitors don't have, which compounds product quality over time.
4. **Proprietary protocol-risk database.** Real, continuously-scored Solana DeFi protocols.
5. **B2B distribution.** A single API lets wallets/neobanks offer an "Earn" product and keep their
   customer — Norvex earns a revenue share. This is the scalable wedge (and the regulatory wedge: the
   partner can hold the license).
6. **Multi-agent, auditable architecture.** Bull/Bear/PM debate + a regulatory-grade decision audit trail.
